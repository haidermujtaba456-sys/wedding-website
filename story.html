How We First Met

❀

It was a family introduction in Lahore — the kind of meeting that begins with formal greetings and ends with everyone forgetting the time. Hassan was nervous. The Bride was gracious. Within minutes, the nervousness gave way to laughter.

There was no grand gesture that day, only a simple, honest conversation. We talked about family, about food, about Lahore in winter — and about the kind of life we hoped to build. By the end of the evening, both families knew: this was worth pursuing with care.

Our First Date

❀

Our first proper date was simple on purpose: chai, a long walk, and a conversation with no rush. Hassan arrived early. The Bride arrived exactly on time. We talked until the chai went cold — and then ordered more.

That afternoon taught us something important: we did not need noise or spectacle to enjoy each other's company. Quiet honesty was enough.

The Proposal Story

❀

The proposal was not staged for cameras. Hassan asked with the blessing of both families, in the presence of the people who matter most — simply, sincerely, and with a ring that had been chosen with care.

The Bride said yes — and the room erupted. There were tears, laughter, sweets, and phone calls to relatives across Pakistan and beyond. That yes is why you are reading this page.

The engagement was celebrated with both families in Lahore, and the wedding week was set for 15–20 December 2026.
