Mayun

A soft, golden beginning. The Mayun is an intimate family ceremony of blessings, turmeric and quiet joy at the bride's residence in Lahore.

  • Venue: Bride's Residence, Lahore
  • Dress code: Yellow / gold — soft, bright and festive
  • Who is invited: Close family and friends
Wed 16 Dec 2026 • 6:00 PM

Mehndi

Henna, marigolds and music. The Mehndi is the brightest night of the week — intricate henna, colourful dress, dancing and songs that everyone joins.

  • Venue: Royal Palm Golf & Country Club, Lahore
  • Dress code: Green, yellow and bright festive colours
  • Highlights: Henna artists, live music, family dances
Thu 17 Dec 2026 • 7:00 PM

Dholki Celebration

The beat of the dhol, the clap of hands, and songs passed down through generations. Dholki is pure joy — informal, musical and full of laughter.

  • Venue: Royal Palm Golf & Country Club, Lahore
  • Dress code: Vibrant festive wear — bring your dancing shoes
  • Highlights: Dhol, folk songs, open dance floor
Fri 18 Dec 2026 • 4:00 PM

Nikah Ceremony

The sacred heart of the wedding. The Nikah is a serene ceremony of prayer, promises and blessings, surrounded by family and close friends.

  • Venue: Jamia Mosque / Royal Palm, Lahore
  • Dress code: Elegant, modest formal wear — whites, creams and pastels
  • Note: Please arrive by 3:30 PM for seating
Sat 19 Dec 2026 • 7:00 PM

Baraat Ceremony

The main wedding celebration. Hassan Mehdi arrives with his Baraat in a grand procession of music and celebration, welcomed at the Royal Palm for an evening of dinner, blessings and joy.

  • Venue: Royal Palm Golf & Country Club, Lahore
  • Dress code: Formal — sherwani / formal suits and elegant dresses
  • Highlights: Baraat arrival, dinner, stage and photography
Sun 20 Dec 2026 • 7:00 PM

Walima Reception

A warm reception hosted by the groom's family to thank everyone who celebrated with us. Dinner, heartfelt speeches and a final evening of dancing close the week.

  • Venue: Royal Palm Golf & Country Club, Lahore
  • Dress code: Formal reception wear — gold, blush and cream
  • Highlights: Reception dinner, speeches, farewell
